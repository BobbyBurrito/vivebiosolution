The Problem with Traditional Butyl-Based Degreasers

For decades, butyl-based degreasers were the go-to for cutting through tough machining oils, coolants, and heavy grease. While effective, they come with significant drawbacks:

  • Health Concerns: 2-Butoxyethanol is classified as a reproductive hazard, posing risks to workers through inhalation and skin exposure.
  • Harsh Odors: Strong fumes lead to poor air quality and worker discomfort.
  • Disposal and site requirements: Review the SDS, local requirements and your facility procedures before disposal.
