Commercial Kitchen Cleaning: Efficiency Without Harsh Fumes

Published by VIVE BIOSOLUTION | May 24, 2026

In the fast-paced world of commercial kitchens, cleanliness isn't just about appearance—it's about food safety, operational efficiency, and creating a healthy environment for staff and customers. Traditional kitchen cleaners often rely on harsh chemicals that produce strong fumes, posing risks to health and comfort. VIVE BIOSOLUTION offers a smarter approach with bio-powered cleaning solutions designed to tackle tough kitchen grease and grime without the caustic trade-offs.

The Challenges of Commercial Kitchen Cleaning

  • Stubborn Grease: Ovens and grills accumulate layers of baked-on grease that are difficult to remove.
  • Food Safety: Strict regulations require impeccable hygiene.
  • Worker Safety: Exposure to strong chemical fumes can cause respiratory issues.

VIVE BIOSOLUTION Solutions

VIVE-GRILL (Oven & Grill Cleaner): Tackles baked-on grease using D-Limonene bio-solvent, eliminating the need for caustic fumes.

VIVE-DISH (Dish Washing Liquid): Concentrated formula built on biodegradable, plant-derived ingredients. Hard on grease yet easy on hands.
