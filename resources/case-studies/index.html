Field Evaluations & Case Studies

We will publish field results only when we have enough context to make them useful: the soil, surface, cleaning method, product concentration and observed result. We will not manufacture testimonials or performance numbers for marketing.

Why this page is intentionally not full of claims

We are building field evidence as products are evaluated in real applications. A useful case study needs more than a before-and-after photo: it should identify the soil, surface, previous cleaning method where relevant, concentration, dwell time or process, and the observed result.

What we will document

Application

What was being cleaned and in what operating environment?

Soil & Surface

Oil, grease, food soil or general dirt—and the substrate being cleaned.

Method

Product concentration, application method, dwell time and rinse/wipe procedure where relevant.

Result

Observed cleaning outcome, limitations and customer feedback without inventing percentages.

If you operate a manufacturing, maintenance, food-service or facility environment, tell us what you are trying to clean.
