About Vive Biosolution Corporation

WINDSOR-BASED CLEANING PRODUCT MANUFACTURER

Cleaning Products for Industrial & Commercial Operations

Vive Biosolution Corporation is a Windsor-based manufacturer and supplier of industrial and commercial cleaning products for industrial, commercial, janitorial, food-service, and facility maintenance applications.

We started Vive Biosolution to give businesses direct access to practical cleaning products for industrial, commercial, food-service and facility applications. Our products are designed for real working environments — machine shops, restaurants, offices, warehouses, maintenance teams, cleaning contractors, and commercial facilities.

From industrial degreasers to ammonia-free glass cleaners, floor cleaners, all-purpose cleaners, dish soap, grill cleaners, and bulk cleaning solutions, our focus is on dependable performance, local supply, and honest product support.

WHY WE EXIST

Local Supply. Practical Products. Direct Support.

As a Canadian manufacturer based in Windsor, Ontario, we understand the needs of businesses across Windsor-Essex, Chatham-Kent, and Ontario. We offer products in 4L jugs, 20L pails, and bulk supply options for companies that need reliable cleaning products without unnecessary supply chain delays.
