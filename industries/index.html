INDUSTRIES & APPLICATIONS

Cleaning Products Organized by the Work You Do

Different environments create different soils, surfaces and cleaning requirements. Explore the applications below to see which VIVE products may be appropriate to evaluate.

CNC & Machine Shops

Routine cleaning around machining operations often involves cutting-oil residue, coolant carryover, grease, footprints and general shop soils. Product selection should depend on the soil, surface and cleaning method—not a one-size-fits-all claim.

Metal Fabrication

Fabrication environments can accumulate oils, grease, shop dust, fingerprints and general production soils across work areas. The right cleaner depends on the substrate, finish and downstream process.

Automotive & Truck Maintenance

Service bays and maintenance shops deal with oil, grease, tracked soils and routine facility cleaning. A cleaning program may require different products for heavy soils, floors, glass and general surfaces.

Tool & Die

Tool and die operations need practical cleaning around machining, maintenance and facility areas. Oils and grease may require a degreaser, while floors and general surfaces often need separate cleaning products.

Commercial Kitchens

Kitchen cleaning is not one job. Cookline grease, baked-on soils, dishwashing and general surface cleaning can require different chemistry and procedures. Always follow the product label and your site sanitation requirements.

Facility & Janitorial

Facility cleaning works better when each product has a defined job. Floors, glass, general hard surfaces and handwashing have different requirements, so the product line should be selected by application rather than broad claims.
