Cleaning Chemicals for CNC & Machine Shops

Routine cleaning around machining operations often involves cutting-oil residue, coolant carryover, grease, footprints and general shop soils. Product selection should depend on the soil, surface and cleaning method—not a one-size-fits-all claim.

COMMON CLEANING TASKS

Cleaning Needs in CNC & Machine Shops

Machine & Equipment Exteriors

Routine removal of oily shop soils from compatible equipment exteriors and surrounding hard surfaces.

Shop Floors

Cleaning tracked oil, grease and general shop soils from compatible hard-floor surfaces.

Maintenance Areas

Cleaning benches, work areas and maintenance surfaces where the selected cleaner is compatible.

Application matters: Surface compatibility, soil type, concentration, dwell time and cleaning method can change the result. Test products on a small area and review the current label and SDS before broader use.
