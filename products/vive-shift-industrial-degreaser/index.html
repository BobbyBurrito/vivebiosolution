VIVE-SHIFT Industrial Degreaser

A concentrated cleaner for oil, grease and common shop soils encountered in manufacturing and maintenance environments. VIVE-SHIFT is intended for equipment exteriors, suitable hard surfaces and shop-floor cleaning where a water-based industrial degreaser is appropriate.

USE GUIDANCE

Concentrated for Flexible Use

For heavy degreasing, a 1:5 product-to-water dilution is a practical starting point. Cleaning requirements vary with soil load, surface, dwell time and application method, so test a small area before broader use.

Important: Always review the current label and SDS before use. Do not assume compatibility with aluminum, painted surfaces or other sensitive materials without testing.

FAQ

VIVE-SHIFT Questions

What is VIVE-SHIFT designed to clean?

It is intended for oil, grease and common shop soils in manufacturing and maintenance environments, including suitable equipment exteriors, hard surfaces and shop-floor applications.

Can it be used in CNC and machine shops?

Those are target application environments. The exact cleaning method should still be selected based on the soil, surface and equipment manufacturer's requirements.

What dilution should I use?

For heavy degreasing, 1:5 is a practical starting point. Adjust only after testing because cleaning performance depends on the soil load, surface and application method.

Can I test it before changing suppliers?

Yes. Request a sample and tell us what you are cleaning, the surface involved and the product or process you currently use. That gives us a better basis for application guidance.
