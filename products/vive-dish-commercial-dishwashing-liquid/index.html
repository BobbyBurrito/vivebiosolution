USE GUIDANCE

Use the Current Label as the Operating Guide

Dosage depends on water volume, soil load and washing conditions. Use a lower dosage for lightly soiled items and increase as required for heavier grease and food soils. Follow the current product label and workplace procedure.

Important: Review the current label and SDS where applicable. Do not infer disinfecting, sanitizing, food-contact, material-compatibility or safety claims that are not stated in current product documentation.
