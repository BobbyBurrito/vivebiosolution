VIVE-SHIFT

Industrial degreaser for manufacturing and maintenance applications.

VIVE-SHIFT is a concentrated industrial degreaser for oils, grease and common shop soils encountered in manufacturing and maintenance environments. It is intended for applications such as equipment exteriors, suitable hard surfaces and shop-floor cleaning. Always review the current SDS and test surface compatibility before use.

Dilution Options:
• 1:5 for heavy industrial degreasing (1 part : 5 parts water)
• Ready-to-use for extreme buildup and drum cleaning
Application Focus:
  • Oil and grease cleanup
  • Manufacturing and machine-shop maintenance
  • Equipment exteriors and suitable hard surfaces
  • Shop-floor cleaning
  • SDS available for workplace review
  • Manufactured in Windsor, Ontario
